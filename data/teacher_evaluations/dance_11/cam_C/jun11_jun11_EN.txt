During the process of shifting the center of gravity, the center of gravity "tilts" rather than "shifts" - manifested in the fact that when moving the center of gravity from one leg to the other, the upper half of the body tilts in the new direction before the pelvis, forming an incorrect sequence of "upper body driving the lower body". The end of the movement of the center of gravity often exceeds the range of the support surface due to being too fast, and there is a tendency to fall forward or sideways, and the stability of the movement is seriously insufficient.
