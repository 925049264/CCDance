Excellently grasps the typical rhythm characteristics of "fast to hold, slow to push". The level of strength can be further enriched during the "extension" process of pushing the fan - that is, the first half of the pushing fan keeps extending at a constant speed, when pushing to the middle section, it slows down slightly to accumulate strength, and when pushing to the end, the final release is completed with a very slight acceleration, so that the process of pushing the fan has internal tension changes rather than a single uniform movement.
