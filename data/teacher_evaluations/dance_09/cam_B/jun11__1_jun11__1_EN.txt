In moving forward and backward, there is a lack of precise timing coordination between the mopping action of the power leg and the transfer of the center of gravity - when wiping forward, the center of gravity often lags behind the power leg (the power leg has reached the farthest end, and the center of gravity still remains on the rear foot); when wiping backward, the center of gravity is ahead of the power leg (the center of gravity has moved backward, but the power leg has not yet completed wiping the floor). This misalignment results in a lack of overall coordination in mobile mopping.
